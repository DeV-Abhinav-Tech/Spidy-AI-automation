# 🕷️ Spidy Task Automation Assistant — Super Easy User Manual

Welcome to **Spidy Task Automation Assistant**! This guide is designed to be super fun, easy, and simple to understand for anyone—even an 8-year-old student! 🎈

---

## 🌟 Quick Start Guide (3 Simple Steps)

1. **📋 Step 1: Create a Task** — Click the `+ New Task` button to add homework, projects, or fun goals.
2. **🤖 Step 2: Chat with Your AI Helper** — Tell Spidy *"Remind me to do my homework at 5 PM"* or *"What is the weather in Tokyo?"*.
3. **⚡ Step 3: 1-Click Auto-Solve Goals** — Type a big goal like *"Build an AI Website"* and click **Auto-Solve Goal**. The AI breaks it into steps and solves them all for you!

## ➕ Feature 2: How to Add a New Task

Click the **`+ New Task`** button at the top right corner of your screen.

![Create New Task Modal](file:///C:/Users/ABHINAV/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/38f85bce-c7ac-4d69-acdf-2e1508c9ded8/create_task_modal_illustration_1786289539519.png)

### 📌 How to Fill Out a Task (Step-by-Step):
1. **Title**: Type the name of your task (e.g. *"Math Homework Chapter 4"*).
2. **Category**: Select `Study`, `Work`, `Project`, or `General`.
3. **Priority**: Pick `High`, `Medium`, or `Low`.
4. **Due Date & Time**: Set the day and time you want to finish.
5. **Click Save**: Your task appears instantly on your board!

## 🤖 Feature 3: Talking to Your AI Robot Helper

Go to the **AI Assistant** tab to talk to Spidy in plain English.

![AI Assistant Chat Interface](file:///C:/Users/ABHINAV/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/38f85bce-c7ac-4d69-acdf-2e1508c9ded8/ai_assistant_mockup_1786288607720.png)

### 📌 Things You Can Ask Spidy (Examples):
* 💬 *"Remind me to finish Science assignment tomorrow at 4 PM"* ➔ Spidy creates the task!
* 💬 *"Mark Python task complete"* ➔ Spidy checks it off as done!
* 💬 *"List all my high priority tasks"* ➔ Spidy shows your urgent tasks!
* 💬 *"Delete task ID 3"* ➔ Spidy safely removes the task!

> [!NOTE]
> Every time Spidy performs an action, a cyan **Tool Execution Badge** appears showing the exact tool used (`create_task()`, `complete_task()`) and confirmation status!

## ⚡ Feature 5: 1-Click Autonomous Goal Solver

Have a huge project that feels too hard? Spidy will break it down and solve it for you!

![Autonomous Subtask Solver](file:///C:/Users/ABHINAV/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/38f85bce-c7ac-4d69-acdf-2e1508c9ded8/subtask_solving_step_illustration_1786289570103.png)

### 📌 How to Auto-Solve Big Goals (Pointer Guide):
1. **Step 1**: Go to **AI Assistant** tab.
2. **Step 2**: Look at the **AI Task Decomposition** box on the right.
3. **Step 3**: Type your goal (e.g. *"Build an AI-powered portfolio website"*).
4. **Step 4**: Click **Auto-Solve Goal**!
5. **Step 5**: Watch Spidy create 5–7 subtasks, write code and solution steps for each, and mark them all completed!

## 🎯 Feature 7: AI Priority Engine Analyzer

Don't know what task to start first? Let the AI analyze your list!

### 📌 How to Run Priority Analyzer:
1. Go to **AI Assistant** tab.
2. Look for **AI Priority Engine**.
3. Click **Analyze Task Priorities**.
4. Spidy grades your tasks from `1.0` to `10.0` based on due dates and urgency, giving you an exact recommendation of what to do first!
